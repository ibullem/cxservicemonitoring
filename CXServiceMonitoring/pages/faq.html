FAQs

What You Need to Do !

• Click the applicable form from the account opening form section from the navigation bar to access the form.
• Enter the required details for each account opened.
• Submit, and you’re good to go!

Alternatively, you can click here to access the FTE form or click here to access the SCM form.

Why does this matter ?

• Ensures every new account is properly tracked. • Keeps records neat and organized. • Streamlines reporting for a smoother process.

What other pro tip do I need to bear in mind ?

• Keep your records handy, this makes updating effortless. • Only enter new information, no need to repeat past entries.
• If you are making entries onbehalf of someone else, ensure that you enter their DAO and not yours.

How do I track my performance ?

Simple, you can do so through the dasboard menu on the navigation bar. Leaderboard will also be published periodically to show current overall performance.
